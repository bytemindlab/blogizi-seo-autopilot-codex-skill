# Quality checklist

## SEO article
- User intent is answered quickly and comprehensively.
- Original insight or examples; no mass-produced filler.
- Claims verified against sources; no fabricated statistics, quotes, testimonials or experience.
- Metadata title, description, slug and keyword are coherent.
- Headings are logical; no keyword stuffing.
- Internal links only to verified live or known scheduled pages; no broken links.
- No duplication against accessible inventory; disclose incomplete inventory.

## OG image
- Relevant to article and distinct from other campaign images.
- Follows supplied brand colors/style; do not invent a logo.
- Text spelling correct if image includes a headline; prefer short text or no text when uncertain.
- Exact 1200x630 output, supported format, <=2,000,000 bytes.
- Safe crop: no critical elements clipped.
- Uploaded to the correct Blogizi post as `cover`.
- Check actual og:image output when a public preview or published URL is accessible.

## Scheduling
- Explicit user approval for date/time or cadence.
- Confirm blog timezone; distinguish local scheduled time from returned UTC.
- Schedule only after upload and QA.
- Blogizi scheduled post may remain draft; use scheduledAt as the scheduling signal.
- Retry with duplicate checks, do not create multiple posts accidentally.
